## 3.2 Establish a clear hierarchy

Create consistent styles for:

*   Main page headings.
*   Section headings.
*   Product names.
*   Product prices.
*   Product descriptions.
*   Navigation.
*   Buttons.
*   Labels.
*   Captions.
*   Metadata.
*   Error messages.
*   Helper text.

Avoid excessive uppercase text.

Avoid excessive letter spacing.

Avoid using oversized headings everywhere.

## 3.3 Eliminate AI-sounding copy

Audit every customer-facing sentence.

Rewrite content that sounds:

*   Robotic.
*   Generic.
*   Repetitive.
*   Overly promotional.
*   Artificially sophisticated.
*   Unnecessarily descriptive.
*   Like a website-generation template.

Avoid repetitive expressions such as:

*   Discover the ultimate...
*   Explore our curated collection...
*   Everything you need, all in one place.
*   Elevate your everyday experience.
*   Carefully curated for modern living.
*   Designed to redefine your lifestyle.
*   Your ultimate destination for...
*   Experience the perfect blend of...

These phrases are not prohibited individually, but should never be repeated mechanically.

### 3.4 Copywriting principles

Write as an experienced human retail copywriter.

Every sentence should:

1.  Communicate something useful.
2.  Sound natural when read aloud.
3.  Be easy to understand.
4.  Avoid unnecessary adjectives.
5.  Avoid repeating information already visible.
6.  Be appropriate for the page.
7.  Use a consistent brand voice.

Use short sentences.

Prefer meaningful product information over generic marketing.
